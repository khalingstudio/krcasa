Mahaguru Phalgunanda Lingden

मुहिङ्गुम अङ्सिमाङ महागुरु फाल्गुनन्द लिङ्देन

Happy Birth Anniversary 10 November 1885

Mahaguru Phalgunanda Lingden was a spiritual leader and social reformer who guided the Kirat community toward peace, unity, and righteousness. His teachings continue to inspire harmony and moral living.

Spiritual leader Social reformer National figure of Nepal
